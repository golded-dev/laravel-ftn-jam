# Code of Conduct

Be useful. Be direct. Do not be a jerk.

## Expected behavior

- Keep discussion focused on the work.
- Critique code and decisions, not people.
- Assume good intent until reality makes that impossible.
- Make room for clear disagreement.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Bigotry or demeaning language.
- Publishing private information.
- Repeated noise after maintainers have asked you to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, close issues, block users, or ban contributors when behavior makes the project worse to work on.
